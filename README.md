# Quantum-Analytics-Healthcare-Insurance-Data-Analysis-Projects

A collection of healthcare/insurance-focused data analytics projects done with Quantum Analytics, covering car insurance policies, mental health disorder trends, and health insurance coverage. Built with Excel and Power BI to turn healthcare and insurance data into clear, actionable insights.

---

## 📁 Projects in This Repository

### 🚗 [Car Insurance Policies Excel Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/Quantum-Analytics-Healthcare-Insurance-Data-Analysis-Projects/tree/main/Car%20Insurance%20Policies%20Excel%20Project)
An Excel analysis of ~37,500 car insurance policies, breaking down driver demographics, car details, claims, and coverage zones — revealing which drivers and regions carry the most claim risk.

### 🧠 [Global Trends in Mental Health Disorders Power BI Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/Quantum-Analytics-Healthcare-Insurance-Data-Analysis-Projects/tree/main/Global%20Trends%20in%20Mental%20Health%20Disorders%20Power%20BI%20Project)
A Power BI dashboard tracking seven mental health disorders across 231 countries and regions from 1990 to 2017, to see which disorders carry the heaviest average load and how prevalence compares across countries.

### 🏥 [Health Insurance Coverage Power BI Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/Quantum-Analytics-Healthcare-Insurance-Data-Analysis-Projects/tree/main/Health%20Insurance%20Coverage%20Power%20BI%20Project)
A Power BI dashboard comparing uninsured rates across all 50 states and DC before and after the Affordable Care Act, to see whether Medicaid expansion or marketplace access made the bigger difference.
